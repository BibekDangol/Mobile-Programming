Explore Nepal

Pokhara

Pokhara is famous for Phewa Lake, boating, adventure sports, and stunning Himalayan views.

Kathmandu

Kathmandu, the capital city, is rich in culture, temples, and historic landmarks like Boudhanath Stupa.

Lumbini

Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage site, attracting pilgrims worldwide.
